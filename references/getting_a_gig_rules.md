# `cassidoo/getting-a-gig` Rules & 4 Career-Level Packaging Strategies

## 1. Non-Negotiable Formatting Rules (`cassidoo/getting-a-gig`)

1. **Make Your Name BIG:**
   - Centered, `18.5pt - 19.5pt Bold`, pure black (`#000000`), Calibri or Arial.
2. **High-Signal Sub-Header Right Below Name:**
   - Format: `[Target Role] | [Primary License / Core Specialty] | [Secondary Specialty]`
   - Examples:
     - *IT:* `Full-Stack Developer | Software Engineer`
     - *HSE/K3:* `Field HSE Officer | Ahli K3 Umum (Kemnaker RI) | On-Site Safety Supervision`
     - *Finance:* `Accounting & Tax Staff | Certified Brevet A & B | Financial Reporting`
     - *HR:* `HR Generalist & Recruitment Specialist | Psychological Assessment & Industrial Relations`
3. **Remove Generic Objectives — Use a High-Impact Summary:**
   - 3–4 lines maximum. Combine role identity + core regulatory/technical frameworks + top measurable outcome.
4. **Strictly 1 Page (`Pages == 1`):**
   - Verified programmatically via Microsoft Word COM (`doc.ComputeStatistics(2) == 1`).
5. **No Scavenger Hunts & Clean Contact Line:**
   - Single line under sub-header: `[City], Indonesia | [Phone/WA] | [Email] | [LinkedIn] | [GitHub/Portfolio]`.
   - Never include religion, marital status, full street/RT/RW address, or skill percentage bars.
6. **5W+1H Bullet Formula:**
   - Every experience bullet must start with a strong **Action Verb (Bold)** + **What was done & How (Tools/Regulations/Methods)** + **Impact/Metric (Why it mattered)**.

## 2. Standard ATS Section Order

Always use this exact section sequence so chronological ATS parsers (*Workday, Taleo, SuccessFactors, JobStreet, Kalibrr, Dealls*) map 100% of fields accurately:

1. `PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY`
2. `WORK EXPERIENCE` *(or `PROJECT & OPERATIONAL EXPERIENCE` for pure Fresh Graduates)*
3. `LICENSES & CERTIFICATIONS` *(if applicable; otherwise place after Skills)*
4. `SKILLS & [DOMAIN] COMPETENCIES` *(MUST contain the word `SKILLS`)*
5. `EDUCATION` *(Pure heading `EDUCATION` so university degree parsing never collides with organizations)*

## 3. Packaging Strategies for the 4 Career Levels ("Semua Kalangan")

### Level 1: Fresh Graduate (0 Months Formal Work Experience)
- **Summary Strategy:** Position as *"Entry-Level [Role] / Fresh Graduate [Major] dengan pengalaman praktis melalui proyek operasional, magang, dan riset terapan..."*
- **Experience Strategy:** Convert PKL/Internship, Capstone Projects, Thesis Fieldwork, KKN, and Lab Assistant roles into structured entries under `WORK & PROJECT EXPERIENCE`.
- **GPA Rule:** Always display GPA (`GPA: 3.XX / 4.00`) bolded if $\ge 3.00$.

### Level 2: Entry-Level with Months of Experience (1–11 Months Working)
- **Summary Strategy:** **NEVER** write *"dengan pengalaman 3 bulan"* in the Summary. Write *"dengan pengalaman praktis dalam implementasi/pengelolaan..."*
- **Frontend/Field vs. Backend/Corporate Calibration:**
  - Focus 100% on **on-site / daily execution ("Frontend")** rather than claiming corporate policy architecture ("Backend").
  - Combine the current active role (`[Month Year] - Present`) with 1–2 previous internships so the candidate has multiple chronological entries and carries active employment status.

### Level 3: Career Switcher (Cross-Major / Lintas Jurusan)
- **Summary Strategy:** Bridge the academic background with the target role's transferable competencies and recent bootcamp/certification credentials.
- **Experience Strategy:** Rewrite past non-linear jobs using the target industry's operational vocabulary:
  - *Teaching / Education $\rightarrow$ Corporate Training, Public Speaking, Administration & Stakeholder Communication.*
  - *Event / Campus Org $\rightarrow$ Vendor Negotiation, Budget Control, Cross-Functional Coordination, & SLA Execution.*
  - *Retail / F&B $\rightarrow$ Inventory Stock Opname, POS Transaction Accuracy, Customer Retention, & Daily Cash Reconciliation.*

### Level 4: Experienced Professional (1–5+ Years)
- **Summary Strategy:** Explicitly state tenure (`dengan pengalaman X+ tahun`) in the first sentence.
- **Experience Strategy:** Compress multi-page history into 1 dense page (2–4 bullets per role) focused on KPIs, cost savings, revenue growth, SLA improvements, and team leadership.

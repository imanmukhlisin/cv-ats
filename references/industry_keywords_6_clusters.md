# Bilingual ATS Keyword Dictionary Across 6 Industry Clusters (All Majors)

Use both **Indonesian** and **International/English** terms in `SKILLS` and `WORK EXPERIENCE` so the CV passes ATS filters regardless of language configuration.

---

## Cluster 1: Engineering, Manufacturing, Construction, Mining & HSE (K3)
*Target Majors: Teknik (Sipil, Mesin, Industri, Elektro, Kimia, Lingkungan, Pertambangan), K3, Kesehatan Masyarakat, MIPA, SMK Teknik.*

- **Field HSE ("Frontend K3" - Entry/Junior/Site):**
  - `Safety Patrol`, `Workplace Safety Inspection`, `On-Site Supervision`, `Hazard Identification`, `Job Safety Analysis (JSA/JSEA)`, `HIRADC`, `Toolbox Meeting (TBM) / Safety Talk`, `Safety Induction`, `Assertive Communication`, `PPE / APD Compliance Monitoring`, `Unsafe Action & Unsafe Condition (UA/UC)`, `Permit to Work (PTW)` (*Hot Work, Working at Height, Confined Space, Lifting*), `LOTO (Lock Out Tag Out)`, `Inspeksi APAR, Hydrant & P3K`, `Housekeeping 5R/5S`, `MSDS / LDKB`, `TPS Limbah B3`.
- **Corporate HSE ("Backend K3" - Senior/Coordinator):**
  - `Ahli K3 Umum (AK3U) Kemnaker RI (TemanK3)`, `SMK3 (PP No. 50 Tahun 2012)`, `ISO 45001:2018`, `ISO 14001:2015`, `CSMS (Contractor Safety Management System)`, `SMKP Kepmen ESDM 1827/2018`, `Incident Investigation (Root Cause Analysis / SCAT / Fishbone)`, `CAPA Tracking`, `Pelaporan Triwulan P2K3 Disnaker`, `Frequency Rate (FR) & Severity Rate (SR)`, `PROPER KLHK`, `UKL-UPL / AMDAL`.
- **Production, QC/QA, PPIC & Maintenance:**
  - `Quality Control (QC) & Quality Assurance (QA)`, `In-Process Inspection (IPQC)`, `Incoming & Outgoing QC`, `PPIC (Production Planning & Inventory Control)`, `Material Requirement Planning (MRP)`, `Preventive & Corrective Maintenance`, `Root Cause Analysis (PDCA, 5 Whys, Fishbone)`, `Lean Manufacturing, Kaizen & Six Sigma`, `AutoCAD, SolidWorks, SAP PM/MM`, `GMP, HACCP, ISO 22000 & SJH Halal` *(FMCG/Food)*.

## Cluster 2: Business, Administration, Finance, Accounting & Banking
*Target Majors: Manajemen, Akuntansi, Ekonomi, Administrasi Bisnis/Publik, Perpajakan, Sekretaris, Semua Jurusan (Admin/Bank).*

- **General Administration & Operations:**

- **Banking & Financial Services (ODP / Frontliner / Credit):**
  - `Service Excellence & Customer Handling`, `Know Your Customer (KYC) & Prinsip Kehati-hatian`, `Credit Analysis (5C Principle)`, `Cross-Selling & Funding/Lending Product Knowledge`, `Cash Count & Teller Operations`.
